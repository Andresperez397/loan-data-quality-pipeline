Notes

The two sheets overlap: the later release repeats 1-9 December 2010. Matched lines are those repeats; every other line appears in one release only, which is expected for a transactional extract.

Invoices starting with C are cancellations (negative quantity). Codes such as POST, M, D and BANK CHARGES are fees and adjustments, not products (rule K1).

About 23% of lines have no customer ID (rule C2, counted as info): they are guest or wholesale-account sales the source does not attribute.

73 loans that were resolved in the earlier snapshot (paid in full or charged off) are active or undisbursed again in the later one. A resolved loan should not reopen, so these are worth raising with the publisher.

200 of the new loans match a removed loan on approval date, loan amount, borrower ZIP, project state: probably the same loan with a revised key field (SBA guarantee: 120, jobs supported: 81). They are counted, not merged.

Notes

Term (months) is not a reliable approval-time field: SBA rewrites it for some loans after approval. Rule K1 counts non-standard terms, and section 3 shows terms changing between quarters.

Interest rates are missing for most loans approved through FY2009 (rule C4). That is a property of the source, so it is counted as info; a missing rate on a later loan is a warning (rule C6).

Active loans have status EXEMPT because SBA withholds their status under FOIA exemption 4.
